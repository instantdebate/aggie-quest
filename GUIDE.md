# Texas A&M freshman application: fall 2027

For a Texas resident finishing a traditional high school and applying to **College Station**. Requirements reviewed October 13–14, 2026. If the campus, term, citizenship, or schooling situation differs, consult admissions before using this plan.

## Dates

The suggested targets are planning advice. Official portals govern your individual requirements. Avoid assuming that a generic timeline applies to a special program.

## 1. Decide what you are applying for

Write down the campus, entry term, and intended majors. Pick programs you would actually want to attend. Open each department’s application information and check for extra requirements such as auditions, portfolios, or honors applications. Record separate deadlines in your calendar.

**Done when:** you can explain your choices and have a list of any additional program steps.

## 2. Gather your information

Ask your counselor for a transcript to reference and your rank information. Gather your senior schedule and dual-credit details. Create a secure personal folder for application copies and receipts.

Make an inventory of activities, employment, volunteering, family responsibilities, leadership, honors, dates, hours, and accomplishments. Describe your actual contribution rather than simply listing club names. Do not overlook jobs or caring for family members.

**Done when:** you have the records and a usable activity inventory.

## 3. Create ONE application

Choose [ApplyTexas](https://www.applytexas.org) or [Common App](https://www.commonapp.org). Choose Texas A&M University, College Station, fall 2027. Use an email address you check and will retain after graduation. Keep credentials in a password manager.

Texas residents applying through ApplyTexas during October 12–18 can use the published free application week. Outside that opportunity, the normal fee is $75, with waiver options where eligible. Check paid/waived status in AIS after submission.

**Done when:** the correct application is started and you can access it independently.

## 4. Write in manageable sessions

Open the live application and copy its prompts and limits into a private working document. Requirements currently listed by admissions include a personal story essay (750-word maximum), a college-readiness life-event answer (250 maximum), and four short answers (100 each maximum) about goals, majors, A&M, and further education. An additional context answer is optional (250 maximum).

Suggested writing process:

1. Spend 20 minutes choosing specific experiences.
2. Draft one answer without editing every sentence.
3. Explain what happened, your choices, and what you learned.
4. Revise for clarity and remove generic praise or inflated claims.
5. Ask a trusted reader for feedback; keep the voice and judgment yours.
6. Paste into the application and check the form’s actual word limits.

**Done when:** every required writing field is complete and sounds like you.

## 5. Complete scholarships BEFORE submitting

Complete the scholarship questions and provide consent for application data to be used for scholarship consideration. Do not assume admission submission automatically handles every scholarship item. A&M says the scholarship application cannot be revised once submitted.

For freshman scholarship consideration, finish the academic self-report and other required scholarship materials by **December 1**. Specific donors can have additional requirements; Terry applicants, for example, need an additional leadership essay. A resume is not a substitute for completing the scholarship activity fields.

**Done when:** scholarship fields, consent, and any relevant donor requirements are complete.

## 6. Review and submit

Read the entire application, preferably aloud. Check campus, term, majors, dates, activities, writing, and scholarship consent. Save a copy in your secure folder. Submit by December 1 and keep the confirmation.

**Done when:** you have proof of application submission. This is not yet proof that your A&M file is complete.

## 7. Open AIS and finish follow-ups

Watch inbox and spam for the acknowledgement email. Use it to activate NetID and sign into the [Applicant Information System (AIS)](https://applicant.tamu.edu). Follow the actual checklist. Contact admissions if the acknowledgement does not arrive within the expected window.

If using ApplyTexas, complete A&M’s emailed extracurricular follow-up form. Confirm your fee is paid or waived. Resolve any individual residency or other document requests shown by AIS.

**Done when:** you can open AIS, have handled follow-ups, and understand every remaining item.

## 8. Complete and LINK STARS

Use the transcript to enter the academic record accurately. A&M estimates 2–6 hours; split this into sessions and review against the transcript before submitting. After application submission, submit and link the STARS record to A&M, then confirm receipt in AIS.

STARS ordinarily replaces the initial high school transcript. Texas top 10% applicants also need official rank verification. Ask your counselor to coordinate it. Graduates and GED applicants have different transcript rules.

**Done when:** AIS reflects the academic record and any required transcript. Aim for December 1 for scholarship consideration rather than waiting for the December 15 admissions document deadline.

## 9. Handle conditional items and verify completeness

Make a deliberate SAT/ACT submission decision. Scores are optional; if sending them, follow the testing-agency process and allow delivery time. Verify receipt. Engineering applicants should review the engineering process: automatic university admission does not eliminate engineering’s holistic review.

Check every required AIS item. Follow up on missing or unprocessed records. Keep a dated private record of the completed checklist.

**Done when:** AIS confirms the admission file requirements are satisfied, not simply when you clicked Submit in an application platform.

## 10. Apply for aid and keep checking

Complete the **2027–28** [FAFSA](https://studentaid.gov/h/apply-for-aid/fafsa), or TASFA if that is the appropriate application for your eligibility. Coordinate contributor steps with your parent. Select A&M in the official form. Check the university’s financial aid portal for receipt and any additional requests.

Set a weekly 10-minute check-in for email, AIS, and aid requests. Keep another college application moving. After an offer, read its exact campus, program, pathway, and reply instructions. Compare costs with your family before commitments. Follow assigned acceptance, New Student Conference, housing, final transcript, and enrollment steps.

**Done when:** aid receipt is verified and you have a routine for handling new requests.

## A practical first week

- Session 1: confirm programs, start the application, request transcript, list accomplishments.
- Session 2: draft story essay.
- Session 3: draft short answers and scholarship material.
- Session 4: review and submit if ready.
- Session 5: prepare STARS; submit and link after the application.
- As emails arrive: open AIS, settle fee status, complete follow-ups, verify receipt.
